Privacy Policy

Effective: [LAUNCH DATE] · Youmek on iPhone, Android and the web, and this website.

The short version

The Youmek app does not collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What you enter in the app

Your tasks, goals, habits, mood check-ins, breaks, dhikr counts, journal and gratitude notes, prayer and Quran tracking, and settings are saved only in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see them, and they are not sent to any server.

Location

Youmek does not use GPS. You choose a city for prayer times, and prayer times are calculated on your device.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled on your device. No notification server is used.

This website

  • Email signup: if you enter your email to get launch news, it is sent to our form provider, Formspree, and used only to email you about Youmek. Every email has an unsubscribe link, or write to us and we will delete it.
  • Language: the site remembers your language choice in your browser. It loads nothing from other companies and uses no analytics or advertising cookies.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings and tap "Erase all my data", or delete the app. Both remove everything Youmek stored.

Children

Youmek is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Health

Youmek is a planning and wellbeing tool. It is not medical or mental health care and does not diagnose anything.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, syncing or AI features, this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the app will ask before sending any data.
